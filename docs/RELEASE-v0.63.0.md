# solMaxima 0.63.0 · Levels

Your creature's stage is now called a **Level** everywhere in the game, instead of a Form.

## Full "Level N"

- The opening screen's nickname button and arena note, for example "Levels 0–4 · Boss victory earns Level 4."
- The journal, growth bar and the "back to Level N" note after running out of lives.
- Toasts such as "Level 4 awakened · next arena unlocked" and the evolution caption.
- Screen-reader labels.

## Short "LvL N"

Used where space is tight:

- the top status badge and its goal line ("2 gates → LvL 1");
- the small team cards on the opening screen;
- the gold evolution banner, which now reads "LvL 4 AWAKENED" or "LvL 10 LEGEND".

## Other changes

The How to play "Growing" paragraph now describes the current 6-gate arenas:

- In Whisperwood, gates 2, 4 and 6 earn Levels 1–3, and the guardian earns Level 4.
- Skyreach works the same way for Levels 5–7, and its guardian earns Level 8.
- In Stormglass, gate 3 earns Level 9, and the final guardian earns Level 10.

Words that describe the creature's body (for example "a new form takes shape") are unchanged.

## Tests

A new test checks that no "Form N" text is left in the game screens, and that the opening screen and growth bar say Level.
