# Maxima 0.38.0 — Flow, readability, sound and contact fixes

## Player-facing changes

- **Gate transitions:** a roughly 0.82-second focus dissolve accompanies a small camera push along the safe frontal reading ray. Physical sentence/answer pieces unfold into their sockets; text fades in after the first part of the motion, and moving pieces cannot be tapped during entry. Accepted work and completed mechanism animation remain intact. Returning to the path uses a short 0.48-second dissolve and establishes the fixed-behind camera immediately, avoiding a sweep through scenery. Reduced Motion shortens the visual transition and removes folding/push motion.
- **Evolution:** the elemental animation lasts 13 seconds, with an extended orbit, light-masked transformation and unobstructed final hold. Original fanfare music accompanies the existing element-specific sound score. After a brief final caption the game automatically returns to the exact saved checkpoint through the path dissolve. There is no Continue expedition/result panel. Skip and sound controls remain available; failure to load a model still offers recovery. The newly adopted model is reused instead of downloading it again on return.
- **Repair tasks:** the exact faulty word/phrase block has an amber outline and wavy underline, with an accessible repair label. Selecting the correct replacement still requires language understanding. Duplicate words elsewhere are not marked accidentally.
- **Message Perches:** all three independently shuffled questions appear in the upper group; all three answers appear below. Existing Wh highlights, separate contexts, correct connections and close-reading support remain.
- **Enchanted signposts:** the actual sentence template is the prominent upper inscription, with larger/bolder text where space permits. The generic grammar instruction no longer occupies the main plate. Saved older boards derive the template from their existing options without changing answers.
- **Gryffindor Form 5:** calibrated the stored forward axis against its head/spine direction and refreshed its model URL. Exploration now establishes the creature's facing immediately after camp, tasks and evolution, keeping the camera behind it. Mesh, skin, textures and animation payload are retained.
- **Coin chests:** rewards save immediately and animate in the world. There is no reward-summary popup or Continue button. The next chapter resumes automatically; the final chest leads into the guardian. Duplicate opening/reload cannot award coins twice.
- **Coin collection:** all 44 forms publish their actual scaled horizontal body bounds. Pickup checks use those oriented bounds and the body's full vertical reach, including jumps, rather than a small centre point. A tall or wide evolved form can collect a coin that visibly touches its body. Small forms still need to jump to reach high coins.
- **Supply requests:** spoken wording is only “Please select …” followed by the requested items. Older saved request wording is normalised for speech without modifying the saved task.
- **Female narration:** existing local recordings use Kokoro `af_heart`. Browser fallback explicitly prefers known female English voices on Apple, Google and Microsoft systems, instead of choosing the first English voice. Known male defaults are excluded. If no suitable English female voice is available on a device, reading support is used; a browser voice cannot be installed by the game. New vocabulary without local recordings uses this same fallback.
- **Brambleguard:** its forward slam reaches 4.4 units, with a 1.08-radian half-angle (approximately 124° total). The visible warning and damage calculation share the same range and angle.
- **Boss music:** guardian encounters crossfade into a separate original 132-BPM battle loop. Transformation has its own original fanfare. Music is quieter for the English finisher, follows sound/music settings and stops when the page is hidden. Audio unlock is attempted on the initial pointer/key gesture before asynchronous model loading, which matters on phones.

## What was checked

All 469 automated checks passed, and `npm run build` validated the complete prebuilt runtime. The release includes checks for transition settling and camera isolation; all 44 forms' body/coin contacts at different orientations and jump heights; correct/duplicate repair targets; grouped question/answer layouts; old and new signpost templates; simplified requests; female voice preference; music assets; stronger Brambleguard geometry; automatic evolution return; popup-free chest progression; saves and duplicate rewards; and initial audio unlock. Existing curriculum, progression, combat, rigging, animation and task checks remain.

The text-fit audit covers 9,486 sampled labels across all 44 units at Levels 0 and 10, with zero predicted overflows at its 320×408 test size. Browser inspection additionally used the production task markup and projected plate geometry in a 390×844 isolated text fixture for Message Perches, repair and signposts. Tiny scroll arrows caused by font rounding were removed from already fitted labels. Isolated QA fixtures do not touch saves and are excluded from the packages.

**Verification limit:** WebGL remains disabled in the cloud browser. Live 3D appearance, perceived animation/music quality, sustained phone frame rate and physical phone touch behavior were not verified here. Mathematical camera/geometry tests, audio decoding and UI fixtures do not establish those results. Real-phone acceptance is still required.

## Compatibility and deployment

All 44 unit journeys, 20 activity formats, four partners, Relaxed/Challenge modes, existing save keys, accepted work, unlocks and coin balances remain supported. Runtime curriculum/Fluent data files and the reusable language-pack ZIP are unchanged. The only changed creature asset is Gryffindor Form 5's orientation metadata; its binary model/animation data is unchanged.

For manual Netlify deployment, extract `Maxima-v0.38.0-Netlify-Drop.zip` and upload the folder immediately containing `index.html`, `assets` and `data`. For repository deployment use `Maxima-v0.38.0-Source-Netlify.zip`, keeping the complete prebuilt `dist` folder and root `netlify.toml` (build `npm run build`, publish `dist`). The build validates prebuilt output; it does not recreate missing assets.
