# solMaxima 0.60.0 · Visual polish: run tracks, coins, lives and rewards

## Clean-ups

- **The top badge reads in full.** The hearts moved beside "Form", so the goal line ("2 gates → Form 1", "Guardian next") gets the whole width. On narrow phones the hearts become a compact "♥×3" on the goal line.
- **One game font.** Panel titles use the game's display face: the journal, results, toasts, the wardrobe, the shrine, the tutorial and the evolution captions. Body text uses its rounded face. Dark panels have a soft gold trim. The logo wordmark keeps its own style.
- **Arena names fit** on the smallest phones.

## Run tracks with depth

- **Sculpted walls in each area:**
  - Whisperwood: a bushy hedge of leafy mounds on mossy stones, with blossoms, ferns and flower lamps.
  - Skyreach: carved sandstone in running bond, with pillars, striped banners and torches.
  - Stormglass: dark crags with glowing crystal clusters and a light seam.
- **A far layer of big silhouettes** (trees, mesas with sails, crystal towers and floating isles) scrolls at a third of the track's speed, so the world has depth while the track rushes past.
- **Lights along the lane edges** blink in turn faster as the run speeds up, so the edges seem to stream toward the creature.
- **Dust puffs** rise from the creature's feet when it runs on the ground, tinted per area.
- **Low graphics** keeps the look with fewer pieces. Walls cost 2.4–4k triangles per 24 m on full graphics and 1.2–2.5k on Low.

## Coins, lives and rewards

- **Minted coins.** Coins have a raised rim and warm metallic gold, and a star glint flashes as each coin turns its face to the camera.
- **Coin streaks.** Coins collected in quick succession chime a step higher each time on a pentatonic scale. Every 10 in a row pops "+10 streak!".
- **Losing a life** flashes the screen edges red and cracks a heart in two.
- **Winning a life** (a completed word) sends a heart flying from the word banner to the badge.
- **Opening a chest** throws golden rays and a flare at the chest.
- **Ulti crystals** shimmer gently: on the team cards, in the League card and at the shrine.
