# solMaxima 0.61.0 · Rich call-outs, a first-letter hint, a sharp evolution screen

## Call-outs that look the part

The centre-screen call-outs now use the game's display font: "Gate ahead!", "Perfect!", "Stumble!", chase warnings such as "▲ Seed pod", power-up names and the rival message.
- **Toned gradients:** gold for great moments, lime for notices, ember red for danger.
- **Depth:** a thick dark outline, a 3D drop and a soft glow.
- **Motion:** a punchy entrance with a slight tilt, and a light sweep across the letters.
- **Long messages** wrap at a smaller size and stay a little longer.

"+10 streak!" pops share the look.

## The first letter is given in word runs

Words have synonyms (student / pupil), so the word panel now shows the first letter of the answer from the start, set in gold. For example, "öğrenci → EN" starts as **S** _ _ _ _ _ _. The rows of letters begin from the second letter. The given letter counts toward completing the word.

## A sharp evolution screen

- **Full resolution:** the evolution renders at the screen's full sharpness (up to 2× pixels) even on phones that play the game at a lower resolution. The game's resolution returns afterwards.
- **Sky:** a smooth shader gradient with fine dithering (no colour banding).
- **Textures:** the glow sprite is 4× larger, and the light rays are a 512 px texture with softened edges.
- **Fixed:** replaying an evolution from the journal during a run no longer fills the console with fog errors.
