# solMaxima 0.59.0 · Team Ulti on the opening screen, a panel that fits phones

## Team Ulti on the opening screen

Each team card on the opening screen shows that team's live Ulti points for the chosen grade, under its banner, with the Ulti crystal. For example, with a Grade 5 lesson selected, the cards show the Grade 5 Gryffindor, Hufflepuff, Slytherin and Ravenclaw pools. Picking a lesson of another grade shows that grade's pools.

The numbers come from the English League:
- They refresh when the opening screen appears, at most once a minute per grade.
- Until the League has answered, or when it is switched off, the cards show no Ulti line.
- Screen readers hear the Ulti with each team's name.

## The opening panel fits phones without scrolling

On phones the panel is more compact:
- a smaller title and spacing;
- shorter arena cards and team portraits;
- a slimmer lesson button, pace switch and Play button.

On shorter screens, the "Your next adventure" label and the arena note are left out. On tall phones the creature's space above the panel now shrinks as needed, so the whole panel always fits beneath it.

The panel was checked without scrolling at 390 × 664 (iPhone Safari with its toolbars) and 339 × 577 (the same phone with 115% text). It was also checked at 320 × 568, 390 × 760, 393 × 852 and 430 × 932.

## Also

The saved-checkpoint line and the arena cards count gates ("2/6 gates") instead of the old "/20 activities".
