# solMaxima 0.57.0 · Lives, and a clear view on every run

## Lives

Each area starts with **three lives**, shown as hearts on the top badge.

- **On the path,** a life is lost when the creature bumps into an obstacle, falls into a hole, or is hit by a rival or by the chasing guardian. One crash only costs one life: a second knock within 1.5 seconds does not count.
- **In the guardian arena,** the hearts sit next to the creature's strength. A knock-out (strength down to zero) costs one life. The guardian still recovers to its last broken ward.
- **Word runs give lives back.** Collecting the letters of the word (or its Turkish–English counterpart) gives **+1 life**, up to five.
- **With no lives left,** the area starts again from its first gate with a new gate order. The coins collected on that attempt and the Forms earned in it are taken back. Specials bought at the shrine are gone too, since they were paid with those coins. **English progress is kept:** mastered words and the Wordex are never lost. An "Out of lives" card explains what happened.

## A clear view on every run

During a run the creature stays in place while the track slides beneath it. The gate it has just passed therefore stood between the camera and the creature for the whole run: the large slab across the screen in the play-test screenshots. While the track slides, every task gate except the one the run leads to now steps aside, at the moment the run begins. The gates return for the run's last real stretch.
