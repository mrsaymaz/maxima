# Maxima 0.52.0 · Cleaner screens

## Top bar

- One compact badge shows the creature, its Form, a progress bar with "5 gates → Form 1", and the coins, in larger type.

## One message area

- Coaching lines ("Run! Grab the coins in your lane."), notices ("The gate is open!") and gesture hints now share one slot under the top bar, one message at a time.
- Coaching lines fade after about four seconds and come back only when the message changes. Urgent ones stay while they matter, for example during a gate fight.
- Short action call-outs ("Jump!", "Perfect!", "Stumble!") stay in the middle of the screen, because they are feedback on what just happened.
- In the guardian arena, hints use the arena's own message line under the health bar.

## Gate signs

- Words on gate signs are no longer split across lines ("playgrou / nd"). The text shrinks a little further, so even a long word fits whole. A word is split only if it cannot fit even at the smallest size.

## Calmer task panels

- Larger instructions and buttons.
- **Hint** and **More** stay hidden when a task opens. They appear after 15 seconds on the task, or straight after a mistake, so they help without distracting.
- Pieces give gentle feedback: a small pop when a piece or pair is right, a short shake for "try again". Both are turned off when the device asks for reduced motion.

## Journey journal

The long list is now four compact tabs, and the journal opens on the last tab used:

- **Journey:** story, Form progress, area and next goal, and the 20 activity types as a grid (✓ done, outlined = next).
- **Words:** Wordex summary, with accepted steps, corrections and hints as three tiles.
- **Rewards:** coins, wardrobe, and the latest evolution replay.
- **Settings:** controls, graphics, music, vibration, backup export and import, and restarting the unit.

Every earlier entry is still there, only grouped. The run's progress bar is hidden while a menu or the controls tutorial is open.
